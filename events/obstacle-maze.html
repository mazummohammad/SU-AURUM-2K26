About the Event

Obstacle Maze Madness is a fun-filled teamwork challenge where one member navigates a maze blindfolded while teammates guide them using signals and instructions. Communication, trust, and speed are the keys to victory.

📅 Date: 22 December 2025

🕙 Time: 11:30 AM

📍 Venue: Indoor Sports Area

Rules

  • Team size: 3 members
  • One participant will be blindfolded
  • Physical contact with the blindfolded member is not allowed
  • Any violation leads to disqualification
  • Fastest team to finish wins
